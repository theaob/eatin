You are a hole in the street, and you are hungry

Start small: cones, bins and people. Grow wide enough for cars, then trees, then houses, then whole office towers. Get big enough and your hole turns into a glowing black hole that bends the city around it.

Four cities to swallow

  • CityDowntown towers, parks, buses and a stadium.
  • Beach townPalm trees, boardwalks, umbrellas and a lighthouse.
  • Theme parkRides, mascots, food kiosks and a Ferris wheel.
  • Snow villagePine trees, snowmen and an ice castle.

Four ways to play

  • ClassicTwo minutes against 7 rival holes. Most points wins.
  • Clean-upNo rivals. Swallow 35% of the city before the clock runs out.
  • BattleNo second chances. Be the last hole standing.
  • Daily cityA new seeded city every day, the same one for everybody.

Grow, chain, collect

  • Chain bites together for combo multipliers.
  • Grab power-ups: speed, magnet, growth spurt and shield.
  • Ride out round events like rush hour, a parade and golden objects.
  • Earn coins and unlock hole skins: Sunny, Bubblegum, Lava, Frost, Galaxy and Solid gold.
  • Fill your Book with everything you have swallowed, and share a card of your best round.
  • Portrait or landscape, with one-finger drag controls.
