Eat In Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026. Applies to the Eat In Android app (com.onurbaykal.eatin) and the Eat In web game.

Eat In is a single-player arcade game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains the little data the game keeps and where it lives.

What the game stores

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your own device, in the browser or app storage:

  • Coins, the hole skins you own and the one you picked
  • The player name you type in, if any
  • Your records (best score, biggest hole, rounds played, wins, best combo) and your collection book
  • Daily challenge progress
  • Settings such as the last mode, map, time of day, music and sound

This data never leaves your device through the game. We, the developer, cannot see it.

What the Android app sends over the internet

Nothing. The Android app runs fully offline: the 3D engine and fonts are bundled inside it, and the app does not request the internet permission. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal or device data.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's saved progress in your backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings, not by Eat In, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

The web version

When you play Eat In in a web browser, your browser downloads the 3D engine from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and the fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web request, those services receive your IP address and browser details. Their own privacy policies apply: Cloudflare and Google. If the page is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub also receives standard request logs.

When the game runs inside Claude as a Claude artifact, a shared daily leaderboard appears. Posting a score there stores your player name, score, hole width and skin with your Claude user ID, so other players of that artifact can see the board. This feature is hidden everywhere else, including the Android app.

Sharing your result

The share card is drawn on your device. It is only sent somewhere if you choose to share it through your phone's share menu or save it as an image.

Children

Eat In does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's storage in Android settings, deletes all saved progress. In a browser, clearing site data for the game does the same.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
